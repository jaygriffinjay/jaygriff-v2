I don't think this is a controversial opinion, but I'll say it anyway: puppies are the best thing on earth. Not top five. Number one. It's not close.

## They Are Unreasonably Happy to See You

You could leave the room for 45 seconds to get a glass of water and come back to a puppy that acts like you just returned from a six-month expedition. That kind of enthusiasm is rare. Most people can't even muster that energy for things they actually care about. Puppies do it for *you*, specifically, every single time.

## They Have No Agenda

A puppy is not thinking about your flaws. It's not keeping score. It doesn't care what you did last Tuesday or whether you've been productive lately. It just wants to be near you and maybe chew on something it shouldn't. That's a refreshingly simple relationship.

## The Zoomies Are a Gift

At some point, for no discernible reason, a puppy will just start sprinting in circles at full speed. No warning. No explanation. Just pure, unfiltered chaos energy for about 90 seconds, and then it's over. I don't know what causes it and I don't want to know. It's perfect as a mystery.

## They Make Everything Feel Less Serious

Bad day? Puppy. Stressful week? Puppy. Existential dread about the nature of time? Honestly, still puppy. There's something about a small creature that has no concept of your problems and just wants belly rubs that puts everything in perspective pretty fast.

## In Conclusion

Puppies are good. This was not a hard post to write.
