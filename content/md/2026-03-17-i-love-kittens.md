# I Love Kittens

Let me be clear about something: kittens are the best thing on this planet. Not second best. Not top five. The best.

## The Case for Kittens

They're tiny. They're soft. They make little sounds that have no business being as cute as they are. A kitten can be doing absolutely nothing, just sitting there, and it's still the most compelling thing in the room. That's power.

There's also the chaos factor. Kittens are unhinged in the best way. They sprint at full speed for no reason, attack their own tails like they've been personally wronged, and then immediately fall asleep in a bread loaf position. The range. The drama. Unmatched.

## Why They Hit Different

Adult cats are great. I'm not throwing shade at adult cats. But kittens have this specific energy where everything is new and overwhelming and exciting all at once. Watching a kitten encounter a paper bag for the first time is a spiritual experience.

Also, the paws. Tiny little paws that are slightly too big for their bodies. I don't make the rules, that's just objectively wonderful.

## In Conclusion

If you don't love kittens, I don't know what to tell you. I genuinely cannot relate. They are small, chaotic, soft, and perfect, and I will die on this hill.

Kittens forever.
