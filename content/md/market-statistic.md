The S&P 500 has never delivered negative returns over any 20-year rolling period. Never. Not through the Great Depression, World Wars, 2008, or any pandemic or crisis in between. In fact, the longer you hold, the more that probability of losing money approaches zero — by the time you're near that 20-year mark, it becomes virtually impossible.
